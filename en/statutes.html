Statutes of ReviTales

Article 1 – Legal form and public benefit above profit

ReviTales is a public-benefit trust (svěřenský fond) in which the public benefit always takes precedence over profit. All ReviTales land is placed in the trust. The trust’s property belongs neither to the founder, nor to the trustee, nor to the beneficiaries; it is permanently bound to the purpose set out in these statutes.

People who wish to join the mission come together in the ReviTales association (spolek). The association cares for the land under an agreement with the trustee. It does not own the land and therefore can never sell it or otherwise dispose of it.

Any income from activities, such as selling the harvest or holding events, is handled by the association or by a separate company. Its profit serves the purpose of ReviTales in accordance with Article 3.

Article 2 – The land is never for sale

No ReviTales site may ever be sold or otherwise exchanged for money. Every site must serve its beneficial and research purpose.

Article 3 – 20% for charity or development

20% of the profit must be returned to charitable activity or to the development of ReviTales.

Article 4 – Gentle restoration

No site may ever be cut down all at once. Felling must be done very sensitively, ideally by planting several trees of different species for every tree that is felled or falls. As they grow, plants that get in each other’s way are transplanted to a more suitable place. Restoration must be carried out by non-invasive methods without heavy machinery, so that it preserves the natural cycle of nature and does not damage the fragile ecosystem.

Article 5 – Unchangeable statutes

These statutes are unchangeable. Everyone who joins commits to keep them with love for nature and its natural cycles.
